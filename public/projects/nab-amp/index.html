How it works

Three taps in the aisle

01

Scan

Point the camera at any barcode, or type an ASIN or UPC. Eligibility, sales rank, and the live buy box price come back immediately.

02

Check the number

Enter your cost. Net profit, ROI, and max buy price appear for FBA and FBM, with every fee itemised underneath.

03

Log it

Save the item to your Cost Reference sheet. When it sells, NAB Amp matches the cost and writes the realised profit to your Sales Log.

Features

Built for people who source in person

Real Amazon fees, not estimates

Referral and fulfillment fees come from Amazon's Product Fees API at the current live price, so the maths matches Seller Central.

Max buy price, worked backwards

Set the profit you need per unit and NAB Amp tells you the ceiling to pay — the number you actually want standing in a store.

Sales sync to Google Sheets

Shipped orders pull in on demand, dedup automatically, and land in your own sheet with cost and profit filled in. Refunds included.

Two-year backfill

Import your full order history from any start date through the same pipeline. Already-logged sales are skipped, never duplicated.

Multiple buyers

Switch profiles in the header so each person's lookups and costs stay attributed when you source as a pair or a team.

Installs to your home screen

A progressive web app: no app store, opens full screen, and keeps working on the patchy signal at the back of a warehouse.
